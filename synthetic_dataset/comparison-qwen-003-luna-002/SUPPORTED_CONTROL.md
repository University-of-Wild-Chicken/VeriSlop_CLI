Before restarting the corpus experiments, native Qwen completed the full strict CLI for absolute difference of two natural numbers (`difference-v6`). The control is excluded from every corpus benchmark numerator and denominator. It has no paired raw baseline, and its 144 oracle cases are not pooled with the corpus's 1,157 cases.
